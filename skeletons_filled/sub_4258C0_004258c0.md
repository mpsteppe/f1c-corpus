## sub_4258C0_004258c0 — AISteeringStateInit

**Summary:** Resets AI steering dynamics state: sets field at 0x4434 to 0 and field at 0x4438 to 1.0f. If speed < 4.0 or both threshold fields (0x37CC, 0x38C4) > 0.9, sets driverStatus to 2. Called from AIMainUpdate per frame.

**Frame-path:** ON_FRAME_PATH  
**Call frequency:** per-frame tick

**Called by:** AIMainUpdate (00424e70)

| this | 0x38C4 | R | — | second threshold field (compared to 0.9) — grip/traction related |
| this | 0x4434 | RW | 0 | steering dynamics accumulator reset to 0 |

| this | 0x4440 | R | — | Speed [FACT] (also: actualSpeed) |
