## sub_41AD80_0041ad80 — OvertakingEventTracker

**Summary:** Tracks overtaking/passing events between two vehicles. Checks time since last event (2-second cooldown) and distance threshold (>1250.0). Updates per-driver timestamps, distances, and opponent references. Emits events: 114/115 for player-involved overtakes, 225 (with random weather noise gating) or 226 for AI-vs-AI overtakes.
