**Guard interpretation:** Direct-mode guards (==2) on throttle/brake/steering/clutch/handbrake fields bypass the frame-rate-corrected decay/smoothing ramp and directly assign the raw target value. Gear cascade: 0x2424 set to -2 initially, then priority chain from gear 7 down to reverse (0x20F0→0x1FBC) selects the highest active gear. When 0x2424 stays at -2 and NeutralDisplayOverride is 1, output is forced to 0. 0x2428 (gear mode flag) gates the entire gear cascade; when 0 the gear output is not updated this frame.
