## sub_597AA0_00597aa0 — Packed96BitMultiply

**Summary:** Multiplies two packed 96-bit floating-point values (sign+exponent in WORD at +10, mantissa in three dwords at +0/+2/+6). Extracts and adds exponents, computes sign (XOR), multiplies mantissas via 6-term cross-multiplication loop, normalizes result via shift-left/shift-right, rounds with guard bit, and packs result back. Returns the new exponent in EAX.
