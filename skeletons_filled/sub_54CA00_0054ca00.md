## sub_54CA00_0054ca00 — Mesh vertex material transform processor

**Summary:** Processes a mesh's vertex element array, applying material-based UV/texture coordinate transforms to vertex data. Has two paths selected by a flags word: a 16-DWORD vertex stride path with D3D-transformed data, or a 12-DWORD stride path for untransformed data. Part of scene graph mesh rendering pipeline.
