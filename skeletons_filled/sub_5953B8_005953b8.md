## sub_5953B8_005953b8 — ExtendedFloatToOutputFormat (80-bit to 32/64-bit)

**Summary:** Converts an internal 80-bit extended-precision float (stored as word array) to 32-bit or 64-bit IEEE float output. Extracts sign, biased exponent (bias 0x3FFF), mantissa; applies normalization, rounding, overflow/underflow detection. Returns status code (0=normal, 1=range-exceeded, 2=underflow).
