## sub_54D080_0054d080 — Triangle mesh bounding sphere computation

**Summary:** For each triangle in a mesh, computes centroid, bounding sphere radius (max vertex distance from centroid), and dominant normal axis. Handles both 48-byte and 64-byte vertex strides based on MMX flag.

**Frame-path:** ON_FRAME_PATH  
**Call frequency:** per-frame tick
