## sub_59B3B0_0059b3b0 — SurfaceCopyReallocAssign

**Summary:** Copies surface structure fields from source (a2) to destination (this), reallocating pixel buffer and clip-rect array if destination capacity is too small, then copies pixel data and clip-rect data.
