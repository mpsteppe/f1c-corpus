## sub_48B560_0048b560 — InputCharFilterByFieldType

**Guard interpretation:** First checks `CharacterClassificationQuery(a2)` — non-printable chars are BLOCKED early. If mode==2: PASSES only chars that pass sub_589B9E/sub_589B70 or are `.` or `:`; everything else BLOCKED.
