## sub_552910_00552910 — FontGlyphRenderQuad

**Summary:** Renders a single font glyph as a textured quad. Guards on glyph index (a2 < 0x20 or >= glyph count at offset 0x44). Two rendering paths: if bit 0x10000 at offset 0x24 is set, calls ClippedRenderRegion directly; otherwise builds 6 vertices (two triangles) in vertex buffer at offset 0x10 with computed position and UV coordinates from the font texture, advancing the vertex counter at offset 0x8 by 6.
