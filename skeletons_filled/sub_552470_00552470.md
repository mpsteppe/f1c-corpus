## sub_552470_00552470 — FontTextureLoadAndInit

**Summary:** Loads a font texture file, validates the extension, parses dimensions, creates a vertex buffer surface, rounds texture dimensions up to nearest power of two (32-2048), allocates a font texture object, renders the font glyph sheet onto the surface, and replaces the original surface with the font texture's surface. Stores flags in field 0x24.
