## sub_57DE30_0057de30 — ConnectionCleanupDisconnect

**Summary:** Removes a connection node from active linked list under mutex, frees it, clears connection struct fields, resets ring buffer, and reprepends to free list. Coordinates three mutexes for list access, struct field protection, and connection allocation. Calls vtable callback at offset 0x94 after cleanup. Network connection teardown.
