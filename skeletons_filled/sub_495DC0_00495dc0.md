## sub_495DC0_00495dc0 — CarDamageImpactRender

**Summary:** Renders car crash damage impact rectangles. Two modes: state==0 draws a single static damage rectangle at computed position; state==1 draws 80 stepping damage rectangles as an animated crack-spread effect over 800x600 screen space. Uses CollisionNoiseGenerator for per-frame noise and F_10ParamForwarder for rectangle rendering. Part of the car crash damage display system.
