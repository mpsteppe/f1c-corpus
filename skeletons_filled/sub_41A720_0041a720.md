## sub_41A720_0041a720 — PenaltyIncidentEventHandler

**Summary:** Handles penalty/incident events for AI commentary. Maps penalty string IDs (SpeedingInPitL, CutTrack, JumpedStart, IgnoredBlueFlag) to event codes — different codes for player vehicle (151-155) vs AI vehicles (252-256). Inserts event into session event table. For player IgnoredBlueFlag, also fires three Boolean OR checks (123-125).
