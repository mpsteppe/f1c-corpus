**Guard interpretation:** The format/codec identifier at `this+0x0` acts as a state variable controlling all format-specific behaviour:
- `== 20` (raw RGB): PASSES `BitFlagSetter_2319` and `StateFlagSetter_2366(255,1)`, then mutated to 22. Also triggers `AnimationStateFlagSet` if `v49==16`.
- `== 21` (RGB alt): PASSES `BitFlagSetter_2319`. Transitions from 20→21 via `MaskedReadAt8(...,16)`.
- `== 22` (finalised): terminal post-processing state.
- `== 41` (paletted): PASSES the full colour-table / palette construction block (gamma LUT, RGBA remapping). BLOCKED from `sub_5124D5` in the `MaskedReadAt8(...,16)` path.
- `== 50` (default): base format, may transition to 51 or FOURCC.
- `== 51` (compressed): PASSES `DriverStateFlagSet`, BLOCKED from palette path.
- `> 51` (FOURCC, e.g., 909200416 = FOURCC '...A', etc.): dispatch to sub-type 1/3/4 for `ShortComputation_2144` comparison.
- `this+0x40 == 0`: BLOCKED — entire buffer allocation (`OperatorNewNhMallocWrapper`), row-pointer array setup, and palette/colour-table construction are skipped. Function returns 0.
