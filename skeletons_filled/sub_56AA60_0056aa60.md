## sub_56AA60_0056aa60 — BuildConstraintStiffnessMatrixOffDiagonal

**Summary:** Assembles off-diagonal constraint stiffness matrix blocks. For each constraint pair, computes cross products of constraint axes with world-space inverse inertia tensor rows, accumulates into a sparse 3x3 block matrix indexed by constraint IDs. Core of vehicle physics constraint solver setup.
