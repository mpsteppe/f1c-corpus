## sub_540940_00540940 -- Texture Atlas Region Allocator

**Summary:** Allocates rectangular regions in a texture atlas for sprite/texture packing. Creates 36-byte horizontal-region nodes and 28-byte vertical-region nodes, inserting them into sorted linked lists keyed by available space. Supports two allocation dimensions (guarded by bit 0x20 in flags). Returns -1 on validation failure.

Bit 0x20 in flags selects list head (this+1109 vs this+1108) and modifies flags (clears 0x20, sets bit 1) for the alternative dimension.
