**Guard conditions:** Checks FPU status (v6 & 0x4100) after computing squared magnitude of axis vector a2. If magnitude is zero or NaN (flag set): skips entire Rodrigues rotation loop, leaving matrix unchanged, but still proceeds to Euler angle extraction. If magnitude is valid (flag clear): computes normalized axis, sin/cos of angle, applies rotation to matrix.
