**Guard conditions:** Initial scan depends on the format argument a4: a4==8 takes an unconstrained min/max over all 16 floats; any other value constrains the scan to samples in (0,1) and sets v36=1.0 if min==max. For a4==6, samples at/below v37*0.5 or at/above (v36+1.0)*0.5 are skipped (goto LABEL_40) instead of being indexed. Refinement loop breaks early when (v36-v37) < 0.00390625, or when both gradient energies v35*v35 and v34*v34 are < 0.015625; otherwise it runs at most 8 passes. Final endpoints are clamped to [0,1] before being stored.
