## sub_67B620_0067b620 — enable transformation bit 0x1000 (8-bit expansion)

**Summary:** Turns on transformation bit 0x1000 in the png struct's transformations word and returns the struct pointer. Per the info-update logic this flag expands the image to 8-bit samples (palette to RGB/RGBA, adds alpha when tRNS is present, forces low depths to 8); the loader sets it for low-depth gray images and whenever tRNS is present.

**Written-by note (persistence):**
