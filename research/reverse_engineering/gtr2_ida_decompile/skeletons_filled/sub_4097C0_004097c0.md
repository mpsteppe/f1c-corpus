## sub_4097C0_004097c0 — heading solver with jitter, virtual dispatch

**Summary:** Heading/steering solver: samples a probe point via sub_4095D0, resolves an object through sub_4031C0, and computes two difference vectors (object-direction vs. its own position, and probe vs. its own position). It normalizes the direction, flips it and picks a ±π/2 offset depending on the sign of a dot product with the object's axis, folds in a table-sampled jitter angle (index counter dword_BE08A0, scale 0.05, plus an atan2 term), and finally dispatches virtual slot +16 on the object with the probe point and the computed float angle.
