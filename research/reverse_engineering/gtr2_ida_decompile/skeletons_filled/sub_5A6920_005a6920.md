## sub_5A6920_005a6920 — interlocked exchange helper + follow-up call

**Summary:** Low-level atomic helper: builds a 2-dword stack pair {a2, -1}, saves EFLAGS, computes an address from two global counters, restores EFLAGS, atomically exchanges the pair's first dword with that address via _InterlockedExchange, then calls sub_5A6927(a3). Used as the payload-destruction step by the queue-drain routines.
