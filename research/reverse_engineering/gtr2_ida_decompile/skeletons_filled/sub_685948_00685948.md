## sub_685948_00685948 — grid direction/slot assignment with packed result

**Summary:** For node a3 and target row a4, scans up to 4 grid directions (cells at a2+24, rows of 16 bytes keyed by node+0xC) and tries to fill up to 4 slot entries (v29[4] / v25..v28) using candidate cells whose partition matches a4 and whose slot is unused, verifying each with sub_68584A.
