## sub_569FD0_00569fd0 — Destructor: free two dynamic arrays

**Summary:** Teardown routine for a container-style object: zeroes two begin/end (or pointer/count) field pairs at 0x4/0x8, then, if the array pointer at 0xC is set, runs the MSVC vector destructor iterator with 12-byte elements (destructor ~TaskStack) and frees it; same for the 24-byte-element array at 0x14 (destructor sub_568D80). Finally clears 0x14/0x18. Classic `vector<T>::clear/free`-style cleanup.
