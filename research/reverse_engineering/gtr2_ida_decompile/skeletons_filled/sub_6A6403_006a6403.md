## sub_6A6403_006a6403 — UpdateEntriesForParent

**Summary:** Scans objects a2[a3..a4) and their element lists; for every element whose id equals a5 or whose entry parent link (+56) equals a5 it copies the two dwords at a6+12 and a6+16 into the entry's +12/+16 fields, sets *a7 = 1 and remembers the object index.
