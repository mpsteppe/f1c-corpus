## sub_663D59_00663d59 — Recursive node-tree flattener: count slots and fill flag dwords

**Summary:** Recursively walks a scene/node tree (type tag at node+0x4) and lays its leaf ranges into the output dword array a3: chain nodes (type 1) concatenate children, wrappers (types 6, 0xB) forward to a single child, type 8 loops over a child count, and leaf nodes (type 9) claim a2[6]*a2[7] slots which are filled by memset32 with a format-derived flag dword (possibly OR-ing 0x2000000 when the owner's byte at +0x14 has bit 0x20). Returns the total number of slots consumed; unknown node types report "internal error: unknown node".
