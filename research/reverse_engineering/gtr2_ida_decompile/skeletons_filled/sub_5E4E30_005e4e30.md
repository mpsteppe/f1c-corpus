## sub_5E4E30_005e4e30 — project point to screen with viewport clamp

**Summary:** World-to-screen projection: derives a perspective scale from the viewport width/height (0x64/0x68) and the half-FOV terms at 0x7C/0x80, projects the input point through the transform data at this+0x15C (sub_5D7810), divides by depth, then offsets and clamps the result into the viewport rectangle. Outputs the clamped x/y pixel pair through a3.
