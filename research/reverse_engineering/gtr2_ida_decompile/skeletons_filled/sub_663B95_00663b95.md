## sub_663B95_00663b95 — Recursive type-based address resolver for scene/geometry nodes

**Summary:** Recursive resolver that maps a node to an associated address based on its type tag at +0x4: type 6 resolves to the pointer at +0x14 plus 16 bytes, type 13 to the node's own inline data at +0x30, type 12 recurses into its four child pointers at +0x14/+0x18/+0x1C/+0x20 (first non-null result wins), and types 11 or unknown yield 0. Returns 0 for a NULL node.
