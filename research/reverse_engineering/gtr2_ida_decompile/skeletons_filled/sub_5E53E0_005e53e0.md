## sub_5E53E0_005e53e0 — configure viewport rect from scaled inputs

**Summary:** Sets the viewport rectangle fields of `this`: width (0x64) and height (0x68) from a4/a5, the x/y minima (0x78/0x74) from a2/a3, then derives the centers (0x6C/0x70) and the width/height aspect ratio (0x84) and re-runs the projection setup at this+0x7C/0x80 via sub_5E5240. Scaling factors come from the manager object when flag 0x4000 is set, otherwise 1.0; negative conversion results fall back to manager values or zero.

**Called by:** sub_4A21D0 (004a21d0), sub_4D4BC0 (004d4bc0), sub_51D6C0 (0051d6c0), sub_5DF7B0 (005df7b0), sub_5E55E0 (005e55e0), sub_5F0DD0 (005f0dd0), sub_5FF600 (005ff600), sub_604F60 (00604f60)
