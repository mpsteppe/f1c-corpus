## sub_6CE120_006ce120 — Store wrap-block count and pad per-record data

**Summary:** Walks the `this+0x24` records (stride 84 bytes at this+0xDC), computes `count = f1*f2 / this+0x140` and a wrapped remainder `v3 = record[+0x2C] mod (f1*f2)` (replaced by f1*f2 when zero), stores `ceil(v3/count)` into the subobject field at this+0x1F4 for the first record only, then fills `2*count` dwords at (region A buffer selected by the index at this+0x1EC, element v12, offset v3) with repeated copies of the dword just before that position.

**Guard conditions:** the record loop runs only when `this+0x24` (record count) is > 0. The remainder is clamped: when `record[+0x2C] mod (f1*f2) == 0` it is replaced by the full product `f1*f2`. The write to this+0x1F4 happens only on the first iteration (v12 == 0). The fill loop executes only when `2*count > 0` — zero counts leave the record untouched.
