## sub_656C76_00656C76 — 3-byte RGB unpacker to float4 (alpha = 1.0)

**Summary:** Expands a run of 3-byte RGB triplets from the source byte buffer at this[0x20] (offset by a2/a3 strides) into float4 quads — each triplet becomes (byte[2], byte[1], byte[0], 1.0) scaled by 1/255 — then optionally runs the sub_650019 and sub_650286 post-passes over the produced block (gated by this[0x18] and this[0x10]).
