## sub_6B7588_006b7588 — Allocate and initialize bitmap surface planes

**Summary:** Allocates the image/surface plane buffers for a bitmap object: computes a buffer size from width/height/depth fields, allocates the main plane plus optional mask planes selected by flag bits at 0x115, tags each optional plane with its plane index, and recomputes row/stride values (0xC0/0xC4) and saved copies (0x70/0x74). Part of the bitmap/surface setup path.
