## sub_65786D_0065786d — Convert palette-indexed pixel block to float RGBA

**Summary:** Reads this[26] two-byte pixels from the buffer at this[8] (offset by a2/a3 strides): each pixel's first byte indexes a 16-byte float RGBA palette entry pointed to by this[14], whose color is copied to the output, and the second byte is a per-pixel 8-bit alpha scaled by 0.0039215689 (1/255) that overwrites the palette alpha. Afterwards runs up to two optional post-processing passes on the converted buffer, gated by flags this[6] and this[4].
