## sub_5B4672_005b4672 — split 4-float vector into xyz copy and scaled w component (MMX)

**Summary:** Reads a 4-float vector at `a1` (two `__m64` loads). If `a2` is non-null, copies the first three floats (x,y,z) into `a2`. If `a3` is non-null, duplicates the fourth float (w) and writes `w * 2.0` (packed-float MMX multiply by 2.0f) into `*a3`. Ends with `_m_femms` to leave MMX state.

| this | 0x8 | R | — | Floats z (at 0x8) and w (at 0xC) of the vector: z goes into `a2[1].m64_i32[0]`, w is duplicated and multiplied by 2.0 for `*a3`. |
