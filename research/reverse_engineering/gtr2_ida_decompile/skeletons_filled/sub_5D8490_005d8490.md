## sub_5D8490_005d8490 — Quaternion slerp/lerp blend

**Summary:** Interpolates between quaternions `a2` and `a3` with parameter a4 into `this`: computes the dot product, negates `a3` when the dot is negative (shortest path), and either performs spherical interpolation (acos/sin-weighted when `1 − |dot| > 0.02`) or falls back to a plain linear blend. Used by transform/orientation code.

**Guard conditions:** Three branches. (1) `dot >= 0.0` → use `a3` as-is; `dot < 0` → use negated `a3` and the absolute dot (shortest arc). (2) `1.0 − abs(dot) > 0.02` → slerp path: weights `sin(angle·(1−a4))/sin(angle)` for `a2` and `sin(angle·a4)/sin(angle)` for `a3`, where `angle = acos(dot)`. If the dot is within 0.02 of 1.0 (near-parallel quaternions) → BLOCKED from slerp: instead weights collapse to `(1−a4)` and `a4` for a plain linear blend. No further guard — the four output components are always written.
