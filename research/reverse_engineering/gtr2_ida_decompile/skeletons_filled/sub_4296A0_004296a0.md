**Guard interpretation:** Early exit: if `*(this+20472)` (AI active) is 0 or `*(this+20360)` is 0, control jumps to LABEL_21 (no opponent state). The proximity block runs when the distance query result is valid and `> -10.0`; inside, a valid +280 pointer gives the exact distance, else 9999.0, and the standoff value v58 comes from the +280 normal. The abort path runs when distance < 0.5 OR the standoff < 0.0 and, for the approach speed < 2.0, resets the AI (mode = (game state != 6) + 4, sound update, etc.). The history/obstacle walk compares distances against `v58` and picks between the +280 and +276 links; the `this+0x4BB4` (19380) == 1 case is one of the three ways to descend into the +280 target. The stop-path selection at the end depends on `v56` (limit flag), the +20504 byte, the +20548 brake flag and byte_757C08; the `obj+0x3CD8` (state == 3) case resets the AI to the idle/1.0 scale.
