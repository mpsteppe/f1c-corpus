## sub_5C6450_005c6450 — array offset adjuster (8-byte stride variant)

**Summary:** Third variant of the family: walks the array (`this[2]`, count `*this`) subtracting the delta float `v9 = *(float*)(this[2] + 4)` from each element's float at +4 (4× unrolled main loop, then a `for` residual loop), where elements are 8 bytes apart. Afterwards it invokes the virtual method at vtable slot +8 of the sub-object at `this[3]` and sets the byte at `this+0x1E` to 1.

**Guard interpretation:** Two nested gates before any float is modified: count `*this > 0` (else the whole subtraction pass is skipped) and delta `v9 = *(float*)(this[2]+4) > 0.0` (else skipped even with a positive count).
