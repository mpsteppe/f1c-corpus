## sub_6B743C_006b743c — Packed Record Emit (BE32 + native word)

**Summary:** Small serialization helper: converts one 32-bit argument to big-endian in a stack temp and pushes both it and a second 32-bit argument into a sink through sub_67A7FA, then runs two sink operations and returns the last one's result. Belongs to the byte-stream/packing layer used by the 6B74xx–6B7Axx module.

**Called by:** sub_6B74F6 (006b74f6), sub_6B7904 (006b7904)
