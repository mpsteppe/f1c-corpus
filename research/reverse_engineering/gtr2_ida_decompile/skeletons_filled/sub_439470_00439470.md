## sub_439470_00439470 — Attenuation / directional falloff factor

**Summary:** Computes a scalar attenuation factor from two coordinates (a2, a3) using the parameter block at `this`: when this+0x18 != 0 and |atan2(a2, −a3)| < this+0x8, it applies an angle-based boost `(1 − cos(v5 * this+0x10)) * this+0x18 + 1.0`; then multiplies by a directional cosine-power falloff `1 − (1 − (a3²/(a2²+a3²))⁴) * this+0x0`.
