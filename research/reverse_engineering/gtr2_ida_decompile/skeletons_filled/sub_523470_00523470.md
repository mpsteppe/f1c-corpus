## sub_523470_00523470 — Ray-query placement solver (ground height + tilt)

**Summary:** Builds an output placement block a3 from a source position triple a1 and a heading angle a2: copies the position, derives a direction vector (2·sin, 0, 2·cos) from the wrapped angle (a2 − π/2) mod 2π, then performs three height/ray queries via sub_45D190 — centre, negative offset and positive offset, with weights 1.0 then 5.0 — and combines the results into a ground height (a3+4 = mean of both end samples, floored by the centre sample, plus 0.1) and a tilt value (a3+16 = half the difference of the end samples) with a ±0.35 outlier rejection band.

**Guard conditions:** If the first sub_45D190 call returns 0, everything after the position copy and direction build is skipped — a3+4 keeps its copied value and a3+16 remains 0 (BLOCKED). Second call: on success v16 = its result, otherwise v16 falls back to the first result v15. Third call: on success v11 = its result, otherwise v11 falls back to v15. Outlier correction engages only when v11 > (2·v15 − v16) + 0.35 OR v11 < (2·v15 − v16) − 0.35; inside that band the end values are kept as-is. The final height at a3+4 is max(v15, (v16+v11)·0.5) + 0.1 — never below the centre sample plus 0.1.
