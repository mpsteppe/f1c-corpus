## sub_6173C0_006173c0 — buffered string reader with refill-on-demand

**Summary:** Reads a string into a caller buffer (up to MaxCount chars) from an internal buffer at +0x74 that it refills on demand through a virtual method (vtable slot +28): when the read position is the -1 sentinel the buffer is refilled and the position reset, and when the requested span crosses the capacity a refill plus `strcat` wrap-around appends the tail.
