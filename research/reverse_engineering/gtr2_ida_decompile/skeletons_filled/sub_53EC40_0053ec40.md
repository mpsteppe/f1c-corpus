## sub_53EC40_0053ec40 — Outgoing car-state packet builder (network)

**Summary:** Serializes the local car's state into the outgoing network packet: fills vehicle/tyre values, sets/clears individual state bits from the car's flags, dispatches the packet (type 52) with an optional delay, and finally fires the pfnAPC callback with argument 5. A delay equal to the negative float-max sentinel selects the default delay constant.
