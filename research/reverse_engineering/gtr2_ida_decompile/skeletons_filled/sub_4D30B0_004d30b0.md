## sub_4D30B0_004d30b0 — ConditionalRecomputeBounds

**Summary:** Probes two virtual methods of the object (vtable slots +0x4 and +0x8); if either reports empty/zero, derives two values from the arguments a2/a3 through sub_4DF1D0 and stores them at 0x4/0x8. Always forwards both arguments to the string-copy helper (sub_615860) and returns its result for a3.
