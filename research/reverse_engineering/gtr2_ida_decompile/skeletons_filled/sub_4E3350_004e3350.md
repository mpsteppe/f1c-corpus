## sub_4E3350_004e3350 — round up to power of two and store as WORD fields

**Summary:** Computes the smallest power of two ≥ a2 (and likewise ≥ a3) using shift loops, then stores the two results as 16-bit fields at this+0xE and this+0x10. Returns the first computed value. Typical texture/surface dimension normalization for graphics code; called from sub_4D8500.

**Called by:** sub_4D8500 (004d8500)

**Struct fields accessed:**

| Base | Offset | R/W | Init (literal → value) | Purpose |
|------|--------|-----|------------------------|---------|
| this | 0xE | RW | — | 16-bit field (this[7] as _WORD) holding the power-of-two rounding of a2 |
| this | 0x10 | RW | — | 16-bit field (this[8] as _WORD) holding the power-of-two rounding of a3 |

**Written-by note (persistence):**
- this 0xE: Written unconditionally at the end of every invocation (result of the a2 shift loop); not a per-frame recalculation — an external poke survives until the function is called again and overwrites it.
- this 0x10: Written unconditionally at the end of every invocation (result of the a3 shift loop); an external poke survives until the next call overwrites it.

**Guard conditions:** Loop guards only: for a2, `a2 > 1` gates the shift loop (if a2 ≤ 1, result stays 1); iteration continues while `a2 > 1 << v5`, ending at the first power of two ≥ a2. Identical logic for a3 with its own counter. No early return and no other checks — both fields are always written at the end.
