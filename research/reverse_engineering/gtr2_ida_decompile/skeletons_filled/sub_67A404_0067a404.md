## sub_67A404_0067a404 — PNG writer teardown / destroy

**Summary:** Tears down the PNG write struct: ends the zlib deflate stream, frees the compressed-output buffer, six image-row/filter buffers and five filter-heuristic tables, then wipes the 0x19C-byte struct while preserving the jump-buffer region (0x00–0x3F) and the dwords at 0x40/0x44/0x48, which are saved before the wipe and restored after.

**Guard conditions:** No input validation and no early returns — teardown always executes. Each individual free is internally conditional on the buffer pointer being non-null (sub_6B72A2). After the 12 free calls the struct is zeroed over 0x19C bytes; only the first 0x40 bytes (jump-buffer region) and the three saved dwords at 0x40/0x44/0x48 are restored, everything else (mode word at 0x58, zstream pointers, etc.) is left wiped.
