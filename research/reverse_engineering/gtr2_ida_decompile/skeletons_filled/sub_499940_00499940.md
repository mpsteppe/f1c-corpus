## sub_499940_00499940 — LerpPackedBytes

**Summary:** Channel-wise linear interpolation of two packed 32-bit values: clamps the fraction a3 into [0,1], then returns a value whose 4 bytes are each old_byte*(1−t)+new_byte*t (packed value v5 from a1 blended toward a2 by t).
