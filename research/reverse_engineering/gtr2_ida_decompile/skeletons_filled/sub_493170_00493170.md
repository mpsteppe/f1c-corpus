## sub_493170_00493170 — Multiplayer join-game state machine

**Summary:** Drives the client "join game" flow: while a cooldown timer is expired it steps through the room's IP list building "ip:port" strings, waits 4 s between probes, verifies the target's *.GDB is known, then calls CINet::JoinRoom and dispatches on the result. On success it transitions to CLIENT TEAM SELECT; on failure it shows the join-failure page or subscription popups, and finally reports "Server Joined" / "Failed to Join Server" and returns to MoRe.
