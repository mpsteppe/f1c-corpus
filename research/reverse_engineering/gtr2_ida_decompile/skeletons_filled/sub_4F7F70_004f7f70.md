## sub_4F7F70_004f7f70 — flush entries and activate free inner slot

**Guard interpretation:** The entire body is blocked unless bit 0 of this+0xC is clear AND the inner pointer this+0x128 is non-null AND its gate byte +8 is zero; any of these failing returns immediately. The per-entry flush loop then requires this+0x120 > 0 (zero/negative count skips it) and only fires sub_4F7BF0 for entries with byte +11 set. The final slot scan requires inner+0x8C non-null and (inner+0xA0 null OR both inner+0xA0+96 and +116 non-zero); it walks the table while the +116 field is non-zero — the first zero-id entry passes and calls sub_4F59C0, while exhausting inner+0x98 entries with non-zero ids returns without calling it.
