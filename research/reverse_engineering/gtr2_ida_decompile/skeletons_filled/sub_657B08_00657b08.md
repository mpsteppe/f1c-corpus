## sub_657B08_00657b08 — Convert 16-bit luminance pixel block to float RGBA

**Summary:** Reads this[26] 16-bit luminance pixels from the buffer at this[8] (offset by a2/a3 strides); each value is scaled by 0.000015259022 (1/65535) and written to all three color floats with alpha 1.0. Afterwards runs up to two optional post-processing passes on the converted buffer, gated by flags this[6] and this[4].
