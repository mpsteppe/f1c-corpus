## sub_655E66_00655E66 — Vertex packer to 4×16-bit signed shorts (with error diffusion)

**Summary:** Converts one span of float attribute quads (source buffer obtained through optional this[0x54]/this[0x10] pre-passes) into two 32-bit words per element — four signed 16-bit fields clamped to ±32767, packed as (x|y<<16, z|w<<16) — written through the this[0x20] buffer; fractional remainders are diffused into the float buffer at this[0x5C] when set. Traversal direction follows a2's parity and that parity is stored/returned via this[0x7C].
