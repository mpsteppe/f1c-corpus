## sub_417C10_00417C10 — Approximate minimal enclosing sphere (grid search)

**Summary:** Computes the bounding rect (sub_56C0A0), then searches a 5×5×5 grid of offsets (±0.02·extent steps) around the box center for the point that minimizes the maximum distance to every vertex of the selected scene object; the best candidate is written to a2 (center) and its worst-case distance to a3 (radius), starting from the box midpoint and half-diagonal as the initial guess.
