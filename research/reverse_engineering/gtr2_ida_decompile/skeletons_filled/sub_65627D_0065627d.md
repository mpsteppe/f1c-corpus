## sub_65627D_0065627D — Vertex packer to 2×8-bit signed bytes (with error diffusion)

**Summary:** Converts one span of float attribute pairs (source buffer obtained through optional this[0x54]/this[0x10] pre-passes) into a single 16-bit word per element — two signed 8-bit fields clamped to ±127 — written through the this[0x20] buffer; fractional remainders are diffused into the float buffer at this[0x5C] when set.
