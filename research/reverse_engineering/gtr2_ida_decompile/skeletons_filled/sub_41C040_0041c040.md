## sub_41C040_0041c040 — ConfigFile::Load (Config.ini parser)

**Summary:** Loads or creates the GTR2 Config.ini (config.cpp) and parses its [COMPONENTS], [INSTALL], [AUTOLAUNCH] and [VERSION] sections into the application config object — directory paths, video/audio options, grid size, shader level, and autolaunch settings. Called from _WinMain during startup; returns 1 on success, 0 on failure.
