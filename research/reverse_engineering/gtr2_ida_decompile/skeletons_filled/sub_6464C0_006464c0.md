## sub_6464C0_006464c0 — Recursive node destructor

**Summary:** Frees the buffers and child nodes of a node structure, recursing into two child slots so the whole subtree is released. Buffers are only freed when their ownership flag is set; child nodes are destroyed then freed when non-NULL.

**Called by:** sub_64479A (0064479a), sub_6448BC (006448bc), sub_6458B7 (006458b7), sub_6464C0 (006464c0), sub_64651B (0064651b)
