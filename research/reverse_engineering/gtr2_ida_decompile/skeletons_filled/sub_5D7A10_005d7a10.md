## sub_5D7A10_005d7a10 — Iterative 3x3 matrix diagonalization (Jacobi-style)

**Summary:** Numeric routine: initialises `a3` to identity (block zeroed, 1.0 written at 32-byte row stride), seeds `a2` from `a1`'s diagonal (a1[0]/a1[4]/a1[8]), then runs up to 50 sweeps of Jacobi-style rotations on the 3x3 double matrix `a1` (row norm drives a 2/90-based threshold for the first four sweeps), accumulating corrections into `a2`/locals and rotation factors into the `a1`/`a3` blocks.
