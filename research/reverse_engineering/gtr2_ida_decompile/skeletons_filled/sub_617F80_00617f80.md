## sub_617F80_00617f80 — accumulated elapsed-time query

**Summary:** Computes total elapsed seconds from the performance counter: takes the stored counter dwords at +0x18/+0x1C and the accumulator dwords at +0x28/+0x2C, optionally re-reads the live counter when the timer is active and flag bit 0 of +0x48 is set, then evaluates `low/freq + high*(2^32/freq) + seconds-at-+0x10`.
