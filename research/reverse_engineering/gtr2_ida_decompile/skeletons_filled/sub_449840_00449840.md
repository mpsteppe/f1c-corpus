**Guard conditions:** Table loop: the skid value is 0.0 when the record's first double is <= 0.0; otherwise 1-sqrt(...) must exceed 0.66 to produce a nonzero intensity (scaled by 2.941176697457143), and the smoke value requires the same source > 0.39 (scaled by 1.639344223850823). The smoke branch only runs when the smoothed intensity is nonzero AND byte at this+8650 is set AND the double at this+9008 > 0.0. Skid branch: only when the three slip accumulators at this+9056/+9064/+9072 are not all zero, or this+8904 > this+9048 — otherwise the three outputs (a2[41], a2[46], a2[51]) are zeroed. Dirt spawn: only while this+0x18A8 < 0.0 and the slot pointer at this+0x1AC8 is nonzero and its +84 object's byte at +40 is set; spawn only when the squared distance from the wheel exceeds 25.0. Smoke transform: only when the mode word at this+2268 is neither 2 nor 1. The four temperature factors are clamped to [0,1] by the `>= 0.0` / `> 1.0` checks.
