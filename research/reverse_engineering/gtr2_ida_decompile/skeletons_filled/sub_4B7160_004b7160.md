## sub_4B7160_004b7160 — Player (.PLR) list builder

**Summary:** Builds the driver/player list from the `*.PLR` files in the player directory: for each file it allocates a 100-byte player object, reads the player name from the file, appends a list node, sorts the list, and finalizes the player selection state. Part of the front-end player/profile system.
