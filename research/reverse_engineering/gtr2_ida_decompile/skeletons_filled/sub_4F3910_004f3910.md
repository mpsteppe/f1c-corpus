## sub_4F3910_004f3910 — Packed status record apply (bitfield-gated)

**Summary:** Parses a packed flags dword from a2 (bit 30 → enable flag stored at this+8, bits 5–10 → index, sign bit, float at a2+12), and when enabled resets three child objects at 0xD8/0xDC/0xE0 and routes a 0x40-byte record built by sub_4F2340 into per-mode targets. Also maintains a 120-byte-stride array at 0x9C and drains a queue object through its virtual methods.
