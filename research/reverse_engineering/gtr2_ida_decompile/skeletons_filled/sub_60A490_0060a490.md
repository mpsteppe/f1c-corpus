## sub_60A490_0060a490 — Toggle setter for the 0xEC flag with dual notification

**Summary:** Sets the toggle byte at this+0xEC: when the new value differs, it notifies the global manager (vtable+276) on channel 6 with 2 for on / 1 for off using the value at this+0xCC, then notifies channel 5 with that same value, and finally stores the new byte. No action when the value is unchanged.

**Called by:** sub_610E20 (00610e20)

| this | 0xEC | RW | — | Toggle byte (this+236); compared against the incoming a2 and updated only when it differs |

**Written-by note (persistence):**

**Guard conditions:** The entire body PASSES only when `a2 != *(BYTE*)(this+0xEC)`; when equal, nothing is notified and nothing is stored. Inside, a nonzero a2 sends channel 6 value 2 (on); a zero a2 sends channel 6 value 1 (off). The second manager call (channel 5, payload this+0xCC) and the store of a2 into the 0xEC byte are unconditional within the passed branch.
