## sub_67A14C_0067a14c — PNG write-end finalizer

**Summary:** Finishes writing a PNG stream: errors out if no IDAT chunks were ever written, marks the IDAT/end state flag, and delegates final flush/cleanup to sub_6B7BF6. Part of the embedded libpng-style image writer.
