## sub_5C6F60_005c6f60 — Lookup keyframe interval for a time (binary dispatch)

**Summary:** Entry point for locating the keyframe interval containing time a2: reads the count at this+0x0, and depending on whether a2 exceeds the middle key time (this[2] + 8*(count/2) + 4) delegates to sub_5C6790 with either the upper half [count/2, count] or lower half [0, count/2] search range.

**Called by:** sub_5CEE80 (005cee80)

**Struct fields accessed:**

| Base | Offset | R/W | Init (literal → value) | Purpose |
|------|--------|-----|------------------------|---------|
| this | 0x0 | R | — | keyframe count; zero aborts with -1 |
| this | 0x8 | R | — | keyframe array base (this[2]); the middle key's time is read at +8*(count/2)+4 |

**Globals referenced:**

| Address | Name | R/W | Role |
|---------|------|-----|------|

**Functions called:**

| Address | Name | Purpose |
|---------|------|---------|
| 005c6790 | sub_5C6790 | binary-search keyframe interval finder — invoked with the narrowed [lo, hi] range |

**Guard conditions:** count (*this) == 0 → immediately returns -1 (empty track, BLOCKED). Otherwise the middle key time at this[2] + 8*(count/2) + 4 is compared to a2: a2 greater → search upper half [count/2, count]; a2 not greater → search lower half [0, count/2]. The callee returns the found index or -1.
