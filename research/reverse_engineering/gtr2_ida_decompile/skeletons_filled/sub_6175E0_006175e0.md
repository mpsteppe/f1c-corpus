## sub_6175E0_006175e0 — extended state update with re-init on success

**Summary:** Extended version of the state-update routine: applies a4 to the embedded sub-object (vtable slot +4), clears the +0x84 byte, refills content from a2, recomputes the +0x5C flag, and on success re-runs the object's own virtual slot 0 (reset) — forcing the flag argument to 0 when the flag was false; on failure it forces +0x5C to 1. Finishes by re-applying the value to the sub-object, storing it at +0x44, and delegating to sub_6146B0 with the remaining arguments.
