## sub_666305_00666305 — Elementwise 3-operand VM op executor

**Summary:** Elementwise executor for a builtin VM operation taking three lane arrays (a4, a5, a6) and writing the result in place into a3. It selects among three strategies based on the state flags dword at 0x74: a fast path when the a6 ids all have object type bit 1 (and, if low-byte bit 2 is set, are all identical), or one of two generic chained paths (3 or 5 scratch lane buffers) issuing sub_6641F3 opcodes 0x10100000/0x20400000/0x20500000/0x30000000. Returns 0 or a negative error (0x8007000E on allocation failure).
