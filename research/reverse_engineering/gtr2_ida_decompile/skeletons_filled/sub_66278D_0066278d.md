## sub_66278D_0066278d — Expand 2D min/max bounds with products of two points

**Summary:** Computes the four pairwise component products of two 2-component double points (a3, a4: x*x, x*y, y*x, y*y), then folds them into a caller-supplied min/max pair (a2[0] = min, a2[1] = max) initialized from the object's stored bounds at this+0xA0/0xA8 — a 2D interval/bounding-box expansion routine.

**Called by:** sub_662830 (00662830)

| this | 0xA8 | R | — | double index 21 — initial/accumulated maximum bound copied into a2[1] |

**Globals referenced:**

**Functions called:**

| 006343bd | __isnan | tests each of the four product doubles for NaN and branches the handling |

**Guard conditions:** For each of the four products: if it is NaN, then — when a5 & 0x40 == 0 — the product is replaced by 0.0 and accumulation continues; when a5 & 0x40 != 0 — both output bounds a2[0] and a2[1] are set to the NaN product and the routine ends without further accumulation. If no NaN occurs, the function returns after all 4 products. Min/max updates: a2[0] is lowered when a product is smaller; a2[1] is raised when a product is larger.
