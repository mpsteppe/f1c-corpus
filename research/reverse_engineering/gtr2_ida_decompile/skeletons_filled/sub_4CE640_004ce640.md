## sub_4CE640_004ce640 — Reset control state block

**Summary:** Reinitializes a block of control/panel state: nulls the first byte of the buffer reached through the pointer at 0x31C, resets the 0x320 cursor to that pointer, restores capacity constants (0x324=256, 0x32C=16), and rewrites a set of byte flags (0x10C-0x111, 0x213, 0x314, 0x318) to their startup values. Effectively a "revert to defaults" routine for this object.
