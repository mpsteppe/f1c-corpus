**Guard interpretation:** Entry gate: the driver is inert unless `*(_DWORD*)dword_B72314 == 8` (returns 0 otherwise). Byte at 0x1F0 (this+496) == 1 selects the stored-reference comparison `*(float*)(this+500) + 4.0 <= dbl_FB1D18` in place of the `*(float*)(v10+52) >= 6.0` time check, and also clears the reference at +500 when the one-shot at 0x402 fires. Arming additionally requires the +480 and +481 latches both clear; the start-push block runs on the composite condition (mode byte not 0/2, or latch +480 != 1, or latch +481 set, or the start-light comparison, or after sub_528650/sub_583170 the mode byte is nonzero).
