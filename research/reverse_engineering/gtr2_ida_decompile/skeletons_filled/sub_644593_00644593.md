## sub_644593_00644593 — Asymmetric record comparison (returns distance metric or -1)

**Summary:** Compares two records (each holding an index at offset 4 and five DWORD fields from offset 12) by walking both field arrays in lockstep and accumulating an asymmetric difference metric; returns -1 when the index pair is rejected by a lookup table or when the metric fails a self-consistency check. No calls out — pure computation.

**Guard conditions:** Two early-exit guards, both returning -1: (1) `dword_736818[5 * *(a1+4) + *(a2+4)] == 0` — pair not present in the table, function blocked; nonzero entry passes. (2) after the 5-field loop, `v8 == HIBYTE(result)` where v8 counts nonzero a1 fields — this equality (metric high byte matches nonzero-field count) is treated as invalid and returns -1; inequality returns the accumulated `result` metric.
