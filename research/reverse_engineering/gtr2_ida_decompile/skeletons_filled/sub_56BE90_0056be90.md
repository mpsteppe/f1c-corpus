## sub_56BE90_0056be90 — Effect dispatch from two entry-pair flags

**Summary:** Derives two conditions from the first two 68-byte entries at 0x6F8/0x73C (each requires the entry dword nonzero plus its byte at +64 nonzero, and both additionally require the shared byte at 0xBF5), then dispatches an effect helper: if the object at 0x9C0 exists and exactly one condition holds, it calls sub_5FF0C0/sub_5FEE10 with X built as ±fabs(object+0xBC) plus Y/Z from object+0xC0/+0xC4; if neither or both hold it calls sub_5FF0D0. Also drives a no-object dispatch off the pointer at 0x9BC. Called from sub_56E190.

Second dispatch: only when `*(this+0x9C0) != 0` — with v6 only, X = +fabs(obj+0xBC) then sub_5FF0C0(v4) followed by sub_5FEE10(&v7); with v2 only, X = -fabs(obj+0xBC) then sub_5FEE10(&v7) followed by sub_5FF0C0(this+0x9C0); with both or neither, sub_5FF0D0() only.
