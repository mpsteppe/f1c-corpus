## sub_5D7710_005d7710 — Selector-to-bitfield merge

**Summary:** Returns `(a1 & 0xFCFF)` OR'ed with a selector derived from `a3`: mode 1 uses the low 16 bits of field 0x44 from `a1`, mode 2 forces 0x200, anything else passes `a3` through. Packs a small state/flag selector into bits 8–15 of a caller-provided flags word while preserving the other bits.

**Guard conditions:** Branches on `a3`: `a3 == 1` → selector = low 16 bits of `*(WORD*)(a1+0x44)`; else `a3 == 2` → selector = 0x200 (512); otherwise selector = `a3` itself (pass-through). The returned value is always `selector | (a1 & 0xFCFF)` — bits 8 and 9 of `a1` are cleared, all other bits of `a1` pass through.
