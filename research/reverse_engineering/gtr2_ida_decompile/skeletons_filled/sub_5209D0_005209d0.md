## sub_5209D0_005209d0 — deserialize object fields from stream

**Summary:** Deserializes the object from stream a2 (read method called through slot +28 of the stream vtable): reads one header byte, splitting it into this+0 (low 7 bits) and this+0xCC (bit 7), then reads two length-prefixed strings (this+1, this+33, NUL-terminated) and four fixed blocks of 64/64/4/4 bytes (this+65, this+129, this+196, this+200). Always returns 1. Inverse of the serialize routine.
