## sub_664155_00664155 — Truncate element/resource tables and restore snapshot block

**Summary:** Cleanup/reset routine: frees element objects at indices [a2, elementCount) via their deleting destructors (sub_663A9B), frees resource objects at indices [a3, resourceCount) via sub_66238F, zeroes those slots, updates both counts to the cut points, then copies a dword block from a4 into the buffer at this+0x18 sized by *(*(this+0x4)+0x18). Always returns 0.
