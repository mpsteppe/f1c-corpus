## sub_6566CB_006566CB — Luma-alpha packer (A16L16-style) with error diffusion

**Summary:** Over a span of elements this first rewrites each input float triplet in place to its luma (0.2125*R + 0.7154*G + 0.0721*B), then packs luma and the 4th component (alpha) into one 32-bit word — each scaled by 65535 and clamped to 0..65535, luma in the low 16 bits, alpha in the high 16 — written through the this[0x20] buffer; fractional remainders are diffused into the float buffer at this[0x5C] when set. Traversal direction follows a2's parity and that parity is stored/returned via this[0x7C].
