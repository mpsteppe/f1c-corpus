## sub_442160_00442160 — Engine Thermal and Damage Model

**Summary:** Simulates engine thermal dynamics: computes engine/oil/water temperatures from RPM, speed, and ambient conditions; models cooling, overheating damage accumulation, radiator failure events, and triggers "Engine Damaged" HUD message when engine health reaches zero. Uses randomized failure severity tables (flt_7D4054, flt_7D3C54).

**Guard conditions:** Engine damage only accumulates when engine health (offset 0x8280) > 0. When health reaches 0, the failure handler triggers: displays warning, logs event, and randomly assigns sub-system damage values from lookup tables. Overheat threshold (100°C / 373.16K) gates additional thermal stress multiplier.
