**Guard conditions:** No early-return guard; function always builds the timeline. Condition `dword_767598 != 39 && dword_767454[0]` triggers a fixed-temperature override (replaces computed temperature with config value). Night-time window (5h-16h = 28800-57600 seconds into day) uses narrower temperature range (280-400 vs 900).
