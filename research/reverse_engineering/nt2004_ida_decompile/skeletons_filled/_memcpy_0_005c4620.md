**Guard conditions:** Overlap detection: if `a1 > Src` AND `a1 < (char *)Src + Size`, the destination pointer lies within the source buffer (overlapping, dest after src). This condition triggers the **backward-copy path** (DWORDs from end to start, then trailing bytes) to prevent corruption of unread source data. Otherwise (non-overlapping or dest ≤ src), the **forward-copy path** is taken: handles destination misalignment via jump table, then copies DWORDs forward via unrolled switch/case fall-through, then copies trailing bytes.
