## sub_570EA0_00570ea0 — ProcessEventQueueAndRemoveEntry

**Summary:** Mutex-protected event queue processor. Finds a queued entry matching `a2` via linked list traversal, marks its sub-object as complete (byte at +0x28 = 1), removes it from the list, then iterates remaining entries calling per-entry callbacks. Triggers additional processing when global state is 4 or when entry state was 2 and global state is 3.
