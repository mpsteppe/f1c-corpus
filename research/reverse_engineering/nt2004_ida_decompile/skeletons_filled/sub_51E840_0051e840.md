**Guard conditions:** When `this+0x4074 > 1` (head tracking disabled/multiplayer) OR `(obj+0x62D0 & 8) == 0` (game mode flag off), the function exits early. When `byte_769254 != 1` (global head-tracking off), falls through to default path. When `this+0x445C < 0.707` (car speed below threshold), accumulates look-ahead timer; when timer exceeds 5.0s (or 3.0s in pit lane), triggers head-reset and recomputes yaw from velocity. Iterates 4 opponent slots: if >=3 opponents visible and none have been looked at, locks tracking to the lead opponent; if 2+ opponents visible and some unseen, transitions tracking smoothly.
