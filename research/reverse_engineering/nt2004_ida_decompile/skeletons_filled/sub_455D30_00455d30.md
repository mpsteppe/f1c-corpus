## sub_455D30_00455d30 — PublishVehicleTelemetry

**Summary:** Exports vehicle physics telemetry data to global output variables. Reads front suspension values (offsets 0x1148-0x1188), filters them against min/max thresholds (zeroing out-of-range values), and publishes byte flags and equality-check results to diagnostic globals. Repeats for rear suspension (offsets 0x1544-0x1584).
